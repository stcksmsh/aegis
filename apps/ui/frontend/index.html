Aegis Secure USB Backup

Welcome to Aegis

Aegis protects your files by encrypting every backup, so if your USB drive is lost or stolen, no one else can read it. It can't protect you if your computer is already infected, or if someone forces you to unlock your files.

    Security setup

    Your passphrase unlocks the backup. Aegis never stores it on the USB drive.

    Write your passphrase down and keep it somewhere safe, away from the drive. If you forget it, nobody can open your backups, not even Aegis. There is no reset.

    Already used this drive with Aegis on another computer? Type the passphrase you chose back then.

    Use 4+ random words, e.g. "maple-orbit-candle-river".
